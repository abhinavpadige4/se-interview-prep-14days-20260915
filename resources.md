## 📅 Daily Study Tips

### Time Management
- **Pomodoro Technique**: 25 min focus + 5 min break cycles
- **Active Recall**: Test yourself without looking at solutions
- **Spaced Repetition**: Review concepts at increasing intervals
- **Feynman Technique**: Explain concepts in simple terms

### Progress Tracking
- **Daily Log**: Track problems solved and concepts reviewed
- **Weekly Review**: Assess progress and adjust focus areas
- **Error Log**: Track mistakes and review them regularly
- **Confidence Rating**: Rate your confidence in each topic (1-5 scale)
