## Success Tips
1. **Consistency**: Stick to the daily schedule
2. **Active Practice**: Code along with solutions, don't just read
3. **Review**: Spend 10 minutes each day reviewing previous day's material
4. **Mock Practice**: On weekends, try timed problem solving
5. **Health**: Take breaks, stay hydrated, get adequate sleep
